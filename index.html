Why this exists

He stopped meeting me at the door.

Beanie was fourteen. Nothing was wrong on paper — bloodwork fine, no diagnosis, nothing to treat. He had just quietly stopped doing the things that made him him. The stairs became a decision. The greeting at the door became a look up from the rug.

His owner is a compounding pharmacist. He spent a year reading canine trials instead of marketing copy, then blended the first batch on a borrowed bench for one dog. Eleven weeks later Beanie beat him to the door. Every batch since is that formula, published lot by lot.
