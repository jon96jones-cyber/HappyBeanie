Privacy Policy

Effective August 4, 2026 · Happy Beanie · Scottsdale, AZ

This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and what we will never do with it. The short version: we collect what we need to ship peptide chews to your animal and support your subscription — and nothing gets sold to anyone, ever.

What we collect

  • Order information — your name, email, shipping address, and the products you buy.
  • Payment information — processed entirely by Shopify's PCI-certified checkout. Card numbers never touch our servers and we cannot see them.
  • Pet information — the eligibility screener runs in your browser. Your answers about your pet’s age, weight, allergies, medications and diagnoses are used to work out the result and are not stored on our servers or attached to you.
  • Screener reminders — if the screener says your pet is not eligible yet (under twelve months, or pregnant or nursing) you can ask us to check back. We then keep your email address, which of those two reasons applied, whether it is a dog or a cat, and the date you chose — nothing else, and none of your other answers. We send one email on that date and no others. Every one of those emails carries a one-click link that deletes your address, and you can ask us to remove it at any time.
  • Site analytics — basic, aggregated usage data that tells us which pages work.
  • Advertising data — we use the Meta (Facebook) Pixel to measure how our ads perform. It records standard browsing events on this site (pages viewed, items added to cart, checkouts started) and shares them with Meta under its data policy. It never receives your pet's health screener answers, and we never send it your payment details.

How we use it

To fulfill and ship orders, manage recurring subscriptions, answer support requests at the help desk, send order confirmations and (only if you opted in) our newsletter or a screener reminder, and publish lot-level quality data that is never connected to any customer.

What we never do

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not store your pet’s health screener answers, so there is nothing there to share. We share data only with the services required to run the store: Shopify (checkout and order management), our shipping carriers (name and address on the label), our email provider (your address, if subscribed), and Meta for ad measurement as described above.

Your choices

Email hello@happybeanie.com anytime to request a copy of your data, correct it, or delete it entirely. Unsubscribe links live at the bottom of every marketing email. Deleting your data does not affect records we are legally required to keep, such as completed order invoices.

Cookies

We use a session cookie so your cart survives navigation, Shopify's checkout cookies, and the Meta Pixel's advertising cookies for ad measurement. To opt out of Meta's ad targeting, use Meta's Ad Preferences or your browser's tracking-protection settings; industry-wide opt-outs are available at aboutads.info.

Contact

Privacy questions: hello@happybeanie.com.
