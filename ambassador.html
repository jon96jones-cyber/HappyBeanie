The rules

Play it straight. That's the whole rulebook.

Happy Beanie is a supplement brand — what you say about it matters legally, for you and for us. These aren't fine print; they're conditions of the program.

✓
Always disclose. Every post that uses your code or link carries a clear disclosure — #ad, #happybeaniepartner, or the platform's paid-partnership tool.
✓
No health claims we don't make. Share your own pet's story, but never claim Happy Beanie treats, cures or prevents any disease.
✓
Keep your code off coupon sites. Your code is for your audience. Posting it to deal aggregators or coupon sites ends the partnership.
✓
No brand-term ads. Don't run paid ads on "Happy Beanie" or our misspellings, and don't impersonate the brand in handles or domains.
✓
No self-commission. Your code works for your audience, not on your own orders — ask us for your personal ambassador discount instead.
✓
We can part ways. Either side can end the partnership anytime; earned commissions are always paid out on the next cycle.
