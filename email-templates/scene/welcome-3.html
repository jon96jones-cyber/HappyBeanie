Six published studies — three on dogs, three on cats — behind the active ingredients that go into Happy Beanie. None are ours, none were funded by us.

Dogs
 
 3 studies
 
Mushroom blend 01
Dogs given the mushroom compound alone — no chemotherapy — survived longer and their cancer spread more slowly, with the highest dose performing best.
Brown & Reetz · IVC Journal →
 
Elk velvet antler 02
Dogs on elk velvet antler put more weight on their affected limbs and moved better than dogs on placebo, with no adverse effects across the trial.
Research in Veterinary Science · 2014 →
 
Collagen peptides 03
Collagen peptides taken by mouth survived digestion, reached connective tissue, and measurably shifted cells toward rebuilding it rather than breaking it down.
PubMed Central · PMC6789547 →

Cats
 
 3 studies
 
Taurine 04
Cats in heart failure from low taurine recovered normal heart function once taurine was restored — the damage reversed, and taurine became mandatory in cat food.
Pion et al. · Science · 1987 →
 
Green-lipped mussel 05
Cats fed green-lipped mussel and omega-3s were measurably more active than cats on the control diet, tracked by device rather than owner opinion.
Lascelles et al. · 2010 →
 
Type II collagen 06
Cats with degenerative joint disease improved in mobility and daily activity on undenatured type II collagen, confirming the mechanism works in cats and not only dogs.
ResearchGate · 381552574 →
